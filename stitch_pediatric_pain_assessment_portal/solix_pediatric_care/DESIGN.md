## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a welcoming, clear, and reassuring digital environment tailored for non-verbal pediatric care, clinical practitioners, and caregivers. The aesthetic balances clinical authority with gentle, playful accessibility. It combines soft glassmorphism, luminous ambient gradients, and crisp tactile components to foster calm focus while eliminating sensory overwhelm.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Airy, sky-lit gradients evoke openness and optimism, shifting away from sterile, cold medical tools toward an uplifting therapeutic space.
- **Gentle Tactility:** Generously rounded pill geometries, soft shadows, and frosted translucent glass create a safe, non-threatening touch-first environment.
- **Vibrant Accentuation:** Focused hot pink accents and high-contrast navy typography direct visual attention cleanly without straining cognitive load.

## Colors

The palette establishes high readability and soothing tones across light mode interfaces.

### Core Canvas & Accents
- **Canvas Vertical Gradient:** Anchored at the top by Royal Blue (`#0A3CFF`), transitioning softly through Sky Blue (`#5C8DFF`), terminating in Soft Canvas Tint (`#F4F8FF`) at the bottom.
- **Base Surfaces:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and translucent white tiers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`).
- **Primary Interactive Brand:** Royal Blue (`#0A3CFF`) and Sky Blue (`#5C8DFF`).
- **Primary Action Gradient:** Diagonal linear gradient from Hot Pink (`#FF2E93`) to Deep Magenta (`#E0147A`) at 135deg.
- **Deep Neutral Text:** Deep Navy (`#0B1B4D`) for headings, body copy, and high-contrast labels. Subdued copy uses Deep Navy at 60% opacity (`rgba(11, 27, 77, 0.60)`).

### Functional & Clinical Status
- **Optimal / Calm:** Emerald Green (`#2BB673`) for positive observations, calm state metrics, and success feedback.
- **Observation / Mild:** Mild Amber (`#F5A524`) for attention notices, pending status, or mid-level sensory alerts.
- **Urgent / Severe:** Vivid Crimson Red (`#E5484D`) for clinical alerts, high sensory overload triggers, or immediate interventions.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans. Large display titles and section headings utilize lighter font weights (`400` to `500`) to evoke airiness and calm, rather than heavy, intimidating medical blocks. Body and interactive labels leverage slightly higher weights (`500` to `700`) to guarantee instant legibility for clinical caregivers and children in diverse ambient lighting environments.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width bounded at 1360px, 2rem (`gutter-lg`) gutters, 3rem (`margin-lg`) outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid, 1.5rem (`gutter`) gutters, 2rem margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column fluid layout, 1rem (`gutter-sm`) gutters, 1rem (`margin-sm`) margins.

### Spatial Texture
Behind the fluid layouts, render a subtle tech dot-matrix overlay: a geometric dot pattern spaced at 24px intervals with 6% opacity (`rgba(10, 60, 255, 0.06)`) overlaid on the canvas gradient. This anchors translucent cards cleanly in the viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism paired with deep-spectrum ambient light casts instead of harsh grey drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** The gradient ground plane with subtle dot matrix grid.
- **Level 1 (Frosted Cards & Panels):** 
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(11, 27, 77, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 27, 77, 0.02)`
- **Level 2 (Floating Nav & Prominent Modals):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
  - Shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(10, 60, 255, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(11, 27, 77, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Primary Hot Pink Buttons & Focal Accents):**
  - Ambient Glow: `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(255, 46, 147, 0.40), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(224, 20, 122, 0.25)`

## Shapes

The interface is defined by gentle roundedness (`level 3`). Surfaces avoid sharp angles to promote a soft, approachable physical metaphor.

- **Buttons, Badges, & Search Bars:** Complete pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Smooth rounded rectangles sized between `20px` and `28px` (`1.25rem` to `1.75rem`).
- **Interactive Toggles & Sliders:** Full pill forms with inset rounded thumbs.
- **Data Indicator Nodes & Status Bullets:** Perfect circles (`50%`).

## Components

### Top Navigation Bar
- **Form:** Floating horizontal pill suspended 1.5rem below the top viewport edge, centered horizontally.
- **Material:** Frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), enclosed by a hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).
- **Layout:** Centered navigation links flanked by an integrated logo badge on the left and a quick-action profile/status chip on the right. Links feature 0.75rem pill hover states in light sky blue tint (`rgba(92, 141, 255, 0.12)`).

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:**
  - Geometry: Full pill.
  - Fill: Linear gradient (135deg, `#FF2E93` to `#E0147A`).
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, weight 600 (`label-md`).
  - Shadow: Radiant soft hot-pink glow (`0 8px 24px -2px rgba(255, 46, 147, 0.45)`).
  - Hover: Subtle scale transform (`scale(1.02)`) with intensified pink bloom.
- **Secondary Action Button:**
  - Geometry: Full pill.
  - Fill: Translucent white glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`).
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.
  - Text: Deep Navy (`#0B1B4D`), weight 600.
  - Shadow: Soft ambient drop (`0 4px 14px rgba(11, 27, 77, 0.05)`).

### Cards & Containers
- Base radius: `24px`.
- Frosted translucent glass background with inner light reflection (1px semi-transparent white top border highlight).
- Internal padding: `space-xl` (2.25rem).

### Data Visualizations & Gauges
- **Semicircular Score Gauges:** 
  - Background arc: Soft Blue Slate (`rgba(92, 141, 255, 0.15)`) with rounded endpoints.
  - Active metric arc: Vibrant gradient stroke (`#0A3CFF` blending into `#FF2E93`) with a soft matching ambient blur.
  - Value display: Centered display typography with a small status label beneath.
- **Bar Charts:** Pill-capped columns (`border-radius: 9999px`) styled in royal blue with hot pink milestone markers.

### Floating Icon Badges
- Miniature circular floating badges (`36px` to `44px`) positioned offset alongside card titles and section headlines.
- Gradient fills matching either Sky Blue (`#5C8DFF`) or Hot Pink (`#FF2E93`) with crisp white iconography to add gentle, friendly visual landmarks across the UI.

### Chips & Tags
- Small pill elements (`border-radius: 9999px`) with padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Status varieties utilize subtle tinted backgrounds paired with solid status text (e.g., Calm Green at `rgba(43, 182, 115, 0.12)` with `#2BB673` text).

### Form Inputs
- Rounded pill text fields (`border-radius: 9999px`) or `20px` radius for multi-line areas.
- Fill: White translucent field (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), border: `1.5px solid rgba(92, 141, 255, 0.25)`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0A3CFF` with a soft blue ring glow (`0 0 0 4px rgba(10, 60, 255, 0.15)`).
